Sourdough Bread

Welcome to the ultimate guide for making sourdough bread!

There’s something truly magical about baking your own sourdough bread—the crisp crust, the chewy interior, and that unmistakable tangy flavor. Whether you're a seasoned baker or a complete beginner, crafting sourdough from scratch is a rewarding journey that connects you to centuries of tradition. Follow this simple recipe to create your own sourdough bread that everyone will love.

Ingredients

  • 100g active sourdough starter (fed and bubbly)
  • 350g warm water (about 80°F/27°C)
  • 500g bread flour (or all-purpose flour)
  • 10g salt (fine sea salt or kosher salt)

Instructions

    Day 1 - Morning/Afternoon: Mix & Bulk Fermentation

  1. In a large bowl, mix 100g active starter and 350g warm water until dissolved.
  2. Add 500g bread flour and 10g salt, stirring until a shaggy dough forms.
  3. Cover and let rest (autolyse) for 30-60 minutes.
  4. Over the next 4-6 hours, perform 3-4 sets of stretch-and-folds (every 30 minutes):
    1. Wet your hands, grab one side of the dough, stretch it upward, and fold it over.
    2. Rotate the bowl and repeat on all sides.
  5. After the last fold, cover and let the dough rise until puffy, bubbly, and about 50% larger.

    6. Evening: Shape & Cold Proof

  6. Lightly flour a work surface and turn out the dough.
  7. Gently shape into a round (boule) or oval (batard) by folding the edges toward the center.
  8. Place seam-side up in a floured banneton or a bowl lined with a floured towel.
  9. Cover and refrigerate for 8-12 hours (for better flavor and texture).

    10. Day 2 - Morning: Bake

  10. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C) with a Dutch oven inside for 30+ minutes.
  11. Turn the dough onto parchment paper and score the top (~½" deep) with a razor blade.
  12. Transfer to the hot Dutch oven, cover with the lid, and bake for 20 minutes.
  13. Remove the lid and bake 15-20 more minutes until deep golden brown.
  14. Let the loaf cool on a wire rack for at least 1 hour before slicing.
