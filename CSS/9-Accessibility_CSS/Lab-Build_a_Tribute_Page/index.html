Malcolm X

A Freedom Fighter Whose Light Never Dims

Here's a time line of Malcolm X's life:

  • 1925 - Born Malcolm Little on May 19 in Omaha, Nebraska.
  • 1929 - His father, Earl Little, dies after being hit by a streetcar (suspected murder by white supremacists).
  • 1937 - His mother, Louise Little, is committed to a state mental hospital.
  • 1938 - Placed in a juvenile detention home in Mason, Michigan.
  • 1941 - Moves to Boston, Massachusetts, to live with his half-sister, Ella Collins.
  • 1942 - Moves to Harlem, New York City. Begins working various jobs and engages in criminal activities.
  • 1943 - Receives a draft notice for World War II but is classified as "mentally disqualified for military service" after he feigns mental illness.
  • 1944 - Returns to Boston. Continues criminal activities including drug dealing and gambling.
  • 1945 - Leads a burglary ring in Boston.
  • 1946 - Arrested in February for burglary. Sentenced to 8-10 years in prison (Charlestown State Prison).
  • 1948 - Brother Reginald visits and introduces him to the Nation of Islam (NOI) . Begins studying the teachings of Elijah Muhammad.
  • 1949 - Transferred to Norfolk Prison Colony, which has a superior library. Reads extensively.
  • 1952 - Paroled from prison in August. Visits Elijah Muhammad in Chicago. Changes his last name to "X" to symbolize his lost African surname. Becomes a minister of the Nation of Islam.
  • 1953 - Assigned as minister of Temple No. 11 in Boston. Begins gaining followers.
  • 1954 - Becomes minister of Temple No. 7 in Harlem, New York City.
  • 1955 - Continues expanding the Nation of Islam's reach. Meets Betty Sanders (later Betty X).
  • 1956 - Begins dating Betty Sanders.
  • 1958 - Marries Betty X (Betty Sanders) on January 14 in Lansing, Michigan.
  • 1959 - Appears in the CBS television documentary "The Hate That Hate Produced" (March), bringing national attention to the Nation of Islam. Daughter Qubilah is born (December 25).
  • 1960 - Meets Cuban leader Fidel Castro in Harlem. Daughter Attallah is born (November 16).
  • 1961 - Continues speaking nationally. Daughter Ilyasah is born.
  • 1962 - Speaks out after the LAPD shoots and kills NOI member Ronald Stokes (April). Tensions between Malcolm X and Elijah Muhammad begin to grow. Daughter Gamilah is born.
  • 1963 - Learns that Elijah Muhammad has fathered children with several young secretaries, shaking his faith in the leader. On December 1, he comments on President Kennedy's assassination, saying it was "chickens coming home to roost." Elijah Muhammad silences him for 90 days. December – Gives his famous "Our Freedom Can't Wait" speech, calling for immediate action rather than gradual change.
  • 1964 - March 8 – Leaves the Nation of Islam (out of NOI) publicly announces his departure. March 26 – Meets Martin Luther King Jr. for the only time in Washington, D.C. (both were attending a Senate hearing on the Civil Rights Act). April 13 – Begins his Hajj pilgrimage to Mecca. April 19 – Completes the Hajj and converts to Sunni Islam, adopting the name el-Hajj Malik el-Shabazz. May – Returns to the U.S. and softens his previous rhetoric on white people. June – Founds the Organization of Afro-American Unity (OAAU) . Twin daughters Malaak and Malikah are born (September 30).
  • 1965 - February 14 – His home in East Elmhurst, Queens, is firebombed (family escapes unharmed). February 18 – Tells Alex Haley he fears for his life. February 21 – Assassinated at the Audubon Ballroom in Manhattan, New York City while giving a speech. He is shot 21 times. Three NOI members are convicted. He is buried at Ferncliff Cemetery in Hartsdale, New York. October 29 – The Autobiography of Malcolm X is published posthumously.
