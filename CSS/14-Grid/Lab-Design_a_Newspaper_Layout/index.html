Breaking News: Police Uncover Billions Hidden in Secret Safe During Cafe Raid

In a dramatic raid on July 8, 2026, police uncovered a hidden safe containing nearly Rp 60 billion in cash at de'Clan Signature Cafe in Cipete, South Jakarta. The 2x1 meter safe was concealed behind a cupboard and required a specialist lockbreaker to access. Inside, authorities found a staggering SGD 3.13 million, USD 889,965, and IDR 259 million, bringing the total seizure to approximately Rp 60 billion. In addition to the cash, police also seized 74 kg of gold bars from other locations, raising the total value of assets confiscated to around Rp 67 billion. The discovery has sent shockwaves through the public, as the cafe was rumored to be linked to a high-ranking official. Authorities are now actively investigating the origins of the cash flow, working to trace the money's source and uncover the network behind this massive financial operation.
