Breaking: Auto-Translate Glitch Turns Sick Employee Into a Brick

"Boss, I am a Very Sick Brick" - Email Leads to Immediate Firing

by shabri akmal

Jakarta, Indonesia – A 25-year-old IT staff named Dika was fired after an auto-translate glitch turned his sick leave request into a construction confession. He typed "Pak Bos, saya sakit sekali" but the AI translated it as: "BOSS, I AM A VERY SICK BRICK. PLEASE LAY ME DOWN ON A WALL." His boss, thinking he'd lost his mind, fired him immediately. The HR bot replied: "WE DO NOT NEED BRICKS. WE NEED DEVELOPERS."

Dika tried to explain for hours, but every message got worse. "I feel better" became "I AM NOW CEMENT." "It was a misunderstanding" turned into "THIS ROOF HAS NO TILES." The CTO later admitted they accidentally installed a "Construction Mode" plugin. "We'll pay him 3 months salary if he agrees to be our office mascot," the CTO said, laughing. Dika declined. "I just want to be seen as a human. Not a building material," he said.
